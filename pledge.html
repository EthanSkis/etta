Our pledge

Honesty is the whole point.

A check-in service that starts with a small deception isn't one worth having. These are the promises Etta is built on — in writing, before we ever place a call.

Etta promises —

  • Never to pretend to be human. Every call begins with who — and what — Etta is.
  • Never to call without your parent's own consent. They agree before the first call, not after.
  • To stop the moment they say stop. "No more calls, thank you" works instantly, on any call.
  • Never to sell anything. No offers, no promotions, no sponsored anything. Ever.
  • To keep conversations in the family. Summaries go to the people your parent agreed to — no one else.
— Etta
