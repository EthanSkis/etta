Set up Etta

14 days free, and you're not charged until your parent personally says yes to Etta. If they decline, the trial cancels itself and nothing is billed. No hardware, no contracts, cancel anytime — and if your parent ever tells Etta to stop, the calls end and so does the billing.

Add-ons

Only if you need them.

Added or dropped from your family page in a couple of taps, prorated to the day. None of it is ever mentioned to your parent — there is no sales content inside an Etta call, ever.

A second parent $15 / mo

Etta calls both of them, each on their own phone at their own time. Nothing is billed until your second parent gives their own recorded yes — same as the first.

An evening call $19 / mo

A second, lighter check-in at the end of the day. Morning tells you how they started; evening tells you how it actually went.

Monthly care report $9 / mo

The month on one printable page — calls answered, sleep, appetite, spirits, and what came up more than once. Written to be handed to a doctor.

Medication reminders $9 / mo

A short, friendly call at pill time. Etta reminds and asks; she never discusses doses, medicines, or what anything is for. That's for a pharmacist.

Call archive $6 / mo

Keep shared recordings for a year instead of 30 days, and download them. Only ever the calls your parent agreed to share — that's their choice, not yours.

Extra people on the notes $5 / mo each

Beyond the people your plan includes: another sibling, or a care manager who should see every check-in. They can stop the texts themselves at any time.

An occasion call $5 once

A birthday, or a message you'd like passed along — Etta calls on the day you choose and says who it's from. She never pretends to be you. If she can't reach them, you're refunded.

Concierge setup $49 once

A person from Etta calls you, walks you through it, and will stay on the line for your parent's setup call. For when explaining AI to your mother feels like the hard part.

Splitting it with your siblings is free. Invite them from your family page and each pays their own share on their own card; every payment comes straight off your bill. One person carrying the whole cost is how one person ends up quietly resenting the rest.
