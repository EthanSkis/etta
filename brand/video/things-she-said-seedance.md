# "The things she said today" — Seedance 2.0 brand film

Margaret moves around her house on a cordless handset, answering questions we
never hear. Four ordinary answers, and then one that isn't. No text appears
anywhere in the frame, by design — the logo goes on in the edit.

## The audio is unfinished — score the VO in post

**Neither take carries the payoff line.** Measured per-window loudness:

| | speech energy ends | remainder |
|---|---|---|
| Pick | 8.5s | −63 dB to end |
| Alternate | 10.0s | −63 to −90 dB to end |

−63 dB is silence. The line scripted at 00:09–00:12 — *"I haven't really talked
to anybody since Tuesday"* — is not in either file, and that line is the entire
reason the film exists.

This is not worth re-rolling for. Voicing it in post is the better version
anyway: the read is the whole performance, and it should be cast rather than
sampled. The visuals are already cut to hold it.

### VO script and timing

Margaret only. The other end of the line is never heard.

| In | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:01 | "Oh, I slept alright." | Comfortable, unhurried. Nothing is wrong yet. |
| 0:04 | "Just toast so far." | Slightly amused at herself. |
| 0:07 | "Oh, the knee's the knee. Nothing new." | A small dismissive laugh — she is waving it off. |
| 0:10 | "...I haven't really talked to anybody since Tuesday." | Quieter. Lighter. Almost apologetic, like something she hadn't planned to say. |
| 0:12–0:15 | *(silence)* | She listens. Room tone only — do not fill it. |

Keep a bed of room tone under the whole thing so 0:12 reads as held breath
rather than a dropout. The generated ambience is usable for the first half; the
tail needs a room-tone fill.

## Why it is built this way

**The handset, not a smartphone.** It is how that generation actually talks, it
makes the call visible without a screen anywhere in frame, and it quietly
carries the "no app, no device, nothing to learn" promise without a word of
copy.

**The framing widens as it goes.** Close on her hands at the tomatoes, medium at
the window, then wide. She physically shrinks in the frame at the moment she
admits the loneliness. That camera move is the argument.

**Etta is never heard.** Making the AI audible would turn this into a product
demo. Off-mic, the viewer fills in the other end themselves — and what they
fill in is a person.

**Loneliness, not surveillance.** Every competitor sells monitoring. The first
three lines are deliberately boring, because the fourth is the product: not
that someone checked whether she ate, but that someone asked.

## Where it belongs

Homepage, retargeting, and anywhere the viewer already knows what Etta is. It
is not a cold-traffic hook — the payoff arrives at second eleven and a cold
scroller will not wait. Lead acquisition with a disclosure-first or UGC cut and
let this one close.
