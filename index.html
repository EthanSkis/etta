How it works

Three steps. The middle one matters most.

Most services ask the family and start dialing. Etta is different by design: nothing happens until your parent themselves says yes.

1.

You set it up

Tell us about your parent — their name, their interests, the best time to call, and who to contact if a call is ever missed. Takes ten minutes.

2.

They say yes

Before any check-in call ever happens, your parent hears exactly what Etta is — an AI companion — and personally agrees. Often you're sitting right beside them.

Their consent, not just yours
3.

Etta calls, you know

A warm few minutes each day — sleep, meals, medication, whatever they feel like talking about. Afterwards, a short summary lands in your messages.

What you receive

The difference between "alive" and "doing well."

A motion sensor tells you the coffee maker turned on. Etta tells you how she sounded.

  • A note after every call — mood, meals, medication, anything worth knowing, by text or email.
  • Patterns over time — weekly trends in sleep, appetite and spirits, so slow changes don't slip past.
  • If she doesn't pick up — Etta retries, texts her, then works through your chosen contact chain.

Our pledge

Honesty is the whole point.

Etta promises —

  • Never to pretend to be human. Every call begins with who — and what — Etta is.
  • Never to call without your parent's own consent. They agree before the first call, not after.
  • To stop the moment they say stop. "No more calls, thank you" works instantly, on any call.
  • Never to sell anything. No offers, no promotions, no sponsored anything. Ever.
  • To keep conversations in the family. Summaries go to the people your parent agreed to — no one else.

Questions, answered plainly

The things families ask first.

Will my mom know she's talking to an AI?

Yes — always, and proudly. Etta introduces herself as an AI companion at the start of every call. We think trust is the product: a check-in service that starts with a small deception isn't one worth having. Most seniors, once asked honestly, genuinely enjoy having "their" Etta call.

What if she doesn't want the calls?

Then they don't happen. Your parent consents personally before the first check-in call, and can stop the service at any moment just by saying so on a call. Their yes — and their no — always outranks the subscription.

What happens if she doesn't answer?

Etta retries a little later, then sends her a text. If there's still no reply, we work through the contact chain you chose — a neighbor, a sibling, you — so an unanswered phone never just disappears into silence.

Are the calls recorded?

Calls are transcribed to produce the family summary, and your parent is told this plainly when they sign up. Summaries go only to the family members they agreed to. We don't sell data, run ads, or use conversations for marketing — ever.

Is this a medical or emergency service?

No. Etta is a wellbeing and companionship service, not a medical device, and she's no substitute for emergency response. If a call ever suggests something urgent, Etta's job is simple: get the humans who love them informed, fast.
