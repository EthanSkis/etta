What you receive

The difference between "alive" and "doing well."

A motion sensor tells you the coffee maker turned on. Etta tells you how she sounded.

  • A note after every call — mood, meals, medication, anything worth knowing, texted straight to your phone. No app, no inbox, no login — for them or for you.
  • Patterns over time — weekly trends in sleep, appetite and spirits, so slow changes don't slip past.
  • If she doesn't pick up — Etta retries, texts her, then works through your chosen contact chain.
